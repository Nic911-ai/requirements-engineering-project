2. Stakeholder Needs and Concerns

1. Student
- Find equipment available for a required period.
- Submit an equipment booking request.
- Understand whether a request has been approved or rejected.

2. Lecturer
- Request equipment for teaching activities.
- Check whether equipment is available.
- Avoid delays caused by manual booking arrangements.

3. Department Administrator
- Access accurate booking information.
- Support administrative booking activities.
- Reduce manual record-keeping.

4. Equipment Manager
- Manage equipment availability.
- Review equipment bookings.
- Keep track of equipment returns.

5. IT
- Support system availability.
- Assist with technical problems.
- Help protect system information and access.

4. Information Sources

- Students who request equipment.
- Lecturers who use equipment for teaching.
- The Department Administrator who handles administrative booking records.
- The Equipment Manager who oversees equipment availability and returns.
- IT staff who support the college's technical systems.

5. Elicitation Questions

- How do students and lecturers currently request equipment?
- How can users check whether an item of equipment is available?
- Who reviews and approves or rejects equipment booking requests?
- What happens if two users request the same equipment for overlapping dates?
- How are equipment returns and late returns currently managed?



6. Candidate Requirement

- FR-01: The system shall allow users to view equipment availability for a selected date.
- Source: The College Booking System scenario and the identified need to reduce uncertainty about equipment availability.
- Verification: A test user should be able to select a date and view the equipment availability information provided by the system.
  
7. Reflection

Stakeholder identification helped me understand that the equipment booking process affects more than students. Lecturers, administrators, equipment managers and IT staff may also have important responsibilities. Further elicitation is needed to confirm booking permissions, approval responsibilities, equipment return procedures and technical requirements.
